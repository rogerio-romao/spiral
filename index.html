WARNING: Not suitable for photosensitive epilepsy.

Best in full-screen (press F key) and accompanied by your favorite music playlist. If key commands aren't working click on screen first to focus it.

PLAYER: Click Add Track(s) to select one or multiple audio files from your device. Click Add Track(s) again to append more to the playlist. Open expanded playlist view to see, reorder, or remove tracks. Use the controls to play, stop etc. and click on the progress bar to skip. Use the P key to hide / show the player. When a track is finished the next one will play and the playlist will loop.

VISUALIZER: Spiral will do its own thing or you can click, touch or press space bar to create a new spiral. Changes algorithm every 100 seconds by default, press I key to increase by 10 seconds or D to decrease. Or press M to toggle between auto-changing and manual mode. These will take effect after the next change.

The algorithm name is displayed on screen when it changes. Press S for silence mode to toggle it on/off.

H to open / close the help screen.
